**Thread title:** Seedshot (Jam 4): your kills become trees. Is it fun by wave 3?

**Post body:**

Seedshot is my Jam 4 entry (solarpunk + hidden lucky cat). You're a gardener bot in a scrapyard. Every machine you shoot
down roots where it fell and grows into a tree. Trees block bullets (yours and theirs), and your vine can only grab
trees. By wave 10 you're fighting inside a forest you planted.

▶️ Play in the browser, ten waves: https://kingriot.itch.io/seedshot
Password: `jamference`

**Controls**
- Keyboard and mouse: WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to aim, hold left click to shoot seed pods.
- Right click a tree to zip to it. Right click a machine to yank it toward you and tie it up for a moment.
- Phone (landscape): left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires, tap a tree or machine to use the vine.
- P or Esc pauses. M mutes. R or Enter starts again after a run ends.

**Five questions, if you have a few minutes:**
1. By wave 3, did you start choosing *where* to kill machines so the trees ended up where you wanted them?
2. Was anything about the controls confusing in the first 30 seconds? Did you find the vine without being told?
3. Did you find the cat? (No hints yet. Tell me if you gave up.)
4. It has sound now. Was anything too loud or annoying?
5. On iPhone: did the "rotate your phone" message go away when you turned the phone?

Phone or desktop, and which browser, helps a lot. Blunt feedback welcome. Happy to play yours back, drop a link. 🌱
